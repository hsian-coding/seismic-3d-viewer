# Community Standards

Contributors are expected to communicate professionally, critique ideas rather than people, respect scientific uncertainty, and make the project welcoming to participants of different backgrounds and experience levels.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and publication of private information are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when these standards are violated.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers should protect reporter privacy and respond proportionately and consistently.
